SHIPPING POLICY

Fast, nationwide courier dispatches for precision inline gear across India.

1. Coverage & Network

Skate Lab ships high-performance inline skating hardware strictly within India. All order parcels are dispatched through our integrated express courier network (Shiprocket logistics execution blueprint) to safeguard sensitive hardware.

2. Rates & Free Shipping Threshold

FREE DELIVERY: All domestic India orders above ₹500 qualify for Free Express Shipping.

For orders below ₹500, a minimal standard flat delivery fee is calculated at checkout.

3. Dispatch & Transit Timelines

  • Processing Time: Verified UPI orders are packed and dispatched within 24 business hours.
  • Transit Time: Parcels arrive within 7 to 10 Business Days depending on target PIN code courier accessibility.

4. Tracking Updates

Once your package is handed over to the courier service, an active AWB tracking code and direct courier URL are sent directly to your registered WhatsApp mobile number.
