FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS

Key answers for inline hardware selection

What wheel durometer is best for Indian street conditions?

For Indian asphalt and concrete roads, 85A to 88A durometer wheels are optimal. They offer maximum high-rebound shock absorption over rough surfaces while preserving top-end roll speed.

Are these accessories compatible with standard inline skates?

Yes. Our wheels fit standard 608 bearing hubs, and our speed frames support universal 165mm, 195mm, and Trinity mounting distances used by leading inline brands.
